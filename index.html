THE IDEA

One billion pieces of digital space.

Billion Pixel Canvas is a permanent digital canvas containing one billion pixels. Each pixel is offered once for $1.

You can own one pixel, a hundred pixels, a hundred thousand pixels or millions of pixels. The price remains $1 per pixel.

Once a pixel is sold, it is never sold again.
